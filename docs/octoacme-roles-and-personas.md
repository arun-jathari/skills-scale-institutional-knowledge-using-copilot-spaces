# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Lead / Engineering Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide engineering direction, shape technical decisions, and help the team deliver scalable, maintainable solutions that meet product goals.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture, design trade-offs, and engineering standards
- Review implementation quality and technical risks
- Support sprint planning and dependency management
- Mentor engineers and ensure alignment with platform constraints
- Partner with Product and Project Managers on delivery feasibility

### Goals
- Deliver a robust technical solution with clear ownership
- Reduce rework and avoid architectural drift
- Align engineering execution with product priorities

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical design discussions
- Dependency and risk updates during planning and standups
- Escalations for technical blockers and trade-offs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to provide implementation guidance and review quality.
- Aligns with Product Managers on feasibility, sequencing, and technical constraints.
- Supports Project Managers by clarifying delivery risks, dependencies, and timelines.

---

## Security / Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads ensure projects meet required controls, privacy obligations, and secure delivery practices without slowing product progress.

### Responsibilities
- Review features, systems, and processes for security and compliance risks
- Define or validate required controls, approvals, and safeguards
- Coordinate vulnerability triage, remediation, and audit readiness
- Partner with engineering on secure-by-default design decisions
- Help escalate high-risk issues and communicate impact to stakeholders

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across the lifecycle
- Maintain trust and regulatory readiness for delivery
- Support secure, measurable project execution

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews, risk assessments, and remediation updates
- Compliance checkpoints and incident communication
- Escalation paths for privacy, data, and security concerns

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to review implementation choices and identify security issues early.
- Supports Product and Project Managers by highlighting risk implications and required controls.
- Coordinates with Release Managers to reduce operational risk before launch.

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment readiness, release windows, and communication so projects ship smoothly and with clear ownership.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare and validate release plans, schedules, and rollbacks
- Coordinate testing, sign-off, and deployment readiness across teams
- Communicate release scope, risks, and stakeholder expectations
- Track post-release issues and support remediation planning
- Help document launch readiness and operational dependencies

### Goals
- Reduce deployment risk and release instability
- Improve predictability of releases and stakeholder communication
- Enable faster recovery when production issues arise

### Typical Communication
- Release calendars, readiness reviews, and launch checklists
- Deployment status updates and post-release summaries
- Escalations for operational blockers or rollback needs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers and QA/Test Leads to confirm release quality and readiness.
- Works with Project Managers to align timelines, dependencies, and communication.
- Coordinates with Security / Compliance Leads to ensure controls and risk mitigations are complete before go-live.

---

## Customer Success / Support Partner

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support Partners connect delivery work to real customer impact, service quality, and support readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and translate customer feedback into product and process improvements
- Help define support readiness, onboarding expectations, and incident escalation paths
- Prioritize customer-impacting issues and communicate urgency to delivery teams
- Share adoption, usage, and support trends with Product and Project leads
- Support stakeholder communications during launch and rollout phases

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and retention
- Ensure customer-facing readiness before and after launch
- Close the loop between product delivery and support experience

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback cycles, support trends, and adoption updates
- Launch readiness and issue escalation communications
- Cross-functional reviews of customer-impacting risks

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Feeds customer insights to Product Managers to shape roadmap and prioritization.
- Helps Project Managers communicate risks, dependencies, and launch readiness to stakeholders.
- Coordinates with Developers and Release Managers to ensure supportability and issue triage coverage.

---

## Data / Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads define and track the metrics that show whether a project is delivering value, reducing risk, and meeting success criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, dashboards, and reporting needs
- Monitor delivery and product outcomes using objective measures
- Support decision-making with trend analysis and learning loops
- Help validate whether goals are being met after launch
- Partner with Product and PM roles to assess what success looks like over time

### Goals
- Make project health and outcomes visible to the team and stakeholders
- Improve evidence-based prioritization and learning
- Support measurement of value delivered to users and business stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Dashboard reviews, metric summaries, and outcome reporting
- Evaluation updates tied to milestones or releases
- Recommendations on trends, performance, and risks

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides Product Managers with evidence to evaluate feature impact and success metrics.
- Supports Project Managers and PMs with milestone tracking and operational indicators.
- Works with Technical and Release roles to monitor launch quality and downstream business effects.

---

## QA / Test Lead

### Role Summary
QA and Test Leads define the quality bar, validation strategy, and acceptance checks needed to reduce defects and improve confidence before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, critical user journeys, and acceptance validation
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Identify quality risks, defects, and release blockers early
- Partner with Product and Engineering teams on Go/No-Go decisions
- Validate that work meets the Definition of Done and release criteria

### Goals
- Improve confidence in the quality and stability of delivered value
- Reduce defects found late in the cycle
- Support a predictable and trustworthy release process

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, validation results, and defect triage updates
- Release-readiness reviews and approval checkpoints
- Escalations for quality issues and acceptance gaps

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to ensure the implementation is testable and meets acceptance criteria.
- Aligns with Product Managers on expected experience and quality standards.
- Coordinates with Release Managers and Security / Compliance Leads to confirm readiness before launch.

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- For cross-functional initiatives, combine these roles to reflect real team accountability: product intent, delivery execution, quality validation, security and compliance, customer impact, and measurable outcomes.
- This expanded set helps teams clarify ownership, strengthen communication, and improve decision-making across planning, execution, and release.
